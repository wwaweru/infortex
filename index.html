FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Shipping & Delivery
  • Tracking & Support
  • Billing & Documentation
  • What documents are required to ship to Kenya?
    Commercial invoice, packing list, IDF, BL/AWB plus product-specific permits such as KEBS or PPB. We guide you through each requirement.
    How fast do air shipments clear at JKIA?
    With complete documentation and compliant goods, most consignments clear within 1–3 working days.
    Do you handle export shipping from Kenya?
    Yes—airline bookings, AWB/BL issuance, export entries and certificates (origin/inspection) where applicable.
    Sea vs air: which is right for my cargo?
    Choose air for speed and high-value goods; sea suits bulky or cost-sensitive freight. We compare transit times against landed cost.
    Do you deliver to door?
    Yes, we arrange nationwide delivery for containers and loose cargo with secure, GPS-tracked moves.
  • Can you estimate my landed cost?
    Share your invoice, packing list and HS code and we’ll estimate duty, VAT, levies and logistics charges.
    Do you operate at Mombasa, JKIA and ICD Nairobi?
    Yes. We handle port/airport processes at Mombasa Port, JKIA and ICD Nairobi plus inland transport.
    How long does sea freight take?
    Typical ocean transits to Mombasa take 25–40 days depending on origin, carrier and routing.
    FCL vs LCL—what’s the difference?
    FCL is a full container with faster handling and less risk per unit; LCL is shared space, ideal for smaller volumes but allows consolidation time.
    How do I request a quote?
    Tap the WhatsApp button or email info@infortex.co.ke with shipment details for a fast response.
  • What taxes & levies apply?
    Duty (per HS), VAT and government levies such as IDF and RDL. We calculate these precisely for your commodity.
    Can you handle special cargo or permits?
    Yes—pharma, perishables, mining and oversize cargo. We coordinate inspections and permits with KEBS/PPB and relevant agencies.
    Do you operate at Nairobi ICD for inspections?
    We manage ICD processes end-to-end, from inspections and transfers to audit-ready documentation.
    Can you support warehousing & distribution?
    Yes, we offer secure storage, pick & pack, KPI reporting and nationwide distribution.
    What’s the fastest way to get help?
